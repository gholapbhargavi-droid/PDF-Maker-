How to use PDF Maker

  1. Choose Images → PDF or Text → PDF.
  2. For images, select multiple pictures or drag them into the box.
  3. Drag a page card to reorder it.
  4. Choose page size, orientation, fit and margin.
  5. Press Create PDF to download your file.

Tip: For large documents, use reasonably sized images to keep the final PDF smaller.
